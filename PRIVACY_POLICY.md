# Privacy Policy for Oxy

Effective Date: May 25, 2026

Oxy is an attendance tracking application developed by Invatech. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how users can control their data.

By using Oxy, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

Oxy collects only the information needed to provide attendance tracking features.

### Account Information

When you sign in with Google, Oxy may collect and store:

- Name
- Email address
- Google profile photo URL
- Firebase user ID

This information is used to create and manage your Oxy account.

### Student Profile Information

Oxy may collect information entered or selected by the user, such as:

- Semester
- Department
- Roll number
- Attendance tracking start date

This information is used to personalize attendance tracking and organize academic records.

### Attendance Information

Oxy stores attendance data created by the user, including:

- Dates marked as present
- Dates marked as absent
- Attendance history
- Attendance percentage and streak information

This information is used to calculate attendance progress and display attendance history inside the app.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Sign users in securely with Google and Firebase Authentication
- Create and maintain user accounts
- Store student profile details
- Track attendance records
- Calculate attendance percentage, streaks, and history
- Improve app reliability and user experience

We do not sell personal information.

## Data Storage and Security

Oxy uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to store and manage user data securely.

Firebase Authentication is used for Google sign-in. Cloud Firestore is used to store user profile and attendance information.

We take reasonable steps to protect user data, but no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

## Data Sharing

Oxy does not sell, rent, or trade user personal information.

User data may be processed by trusted third-party services used by the app, including:

- Google Sign-In
- Firebase Authentication
- Cloud Firestore

These services are used only to provide authentication, database storage, and app functionality.

## Advertising

Oxy currently does not show advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs.

If advertising is added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before the feature is released.

## Children's Privacy

Oxy is intended for students who can responsibly manage their own attendance records. The app is not knowingly designed to collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law without appropriate consent.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information without proper consent, please contact us so we can review and remove the information if required.

## User Control and Data Deletion

Users can stop using Oxy at any time.

To request account or attendance data deletion, contact us at:

support@example.com

Please include the Google email address used to sign in to Oxy so we can identify the correct account.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
